# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

![Spider-Man](https://example.com/spiderman.jpg)

## About the Movie

**Spider-Man** is a *superhero* story about **Peter Parker**, a teenager from New York City who gains spider-like powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He uses his abilities to protect his city while balancing school, friends, and family.

> "With great power comes great responsibility."


--

## Why I Like It

- Relatable hero who is an ordinary student
- Exciting action and web-swinging scenes
- Strong lessons about responsibility
